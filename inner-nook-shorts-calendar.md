# Inner Nook: 31-Day Shorts Calendar

A 31-day plan you can start on **any Thursday**. The weekday themes stay the same whatever the start date. One Short a day, **31 in total**, drawn from `inner-nook-shorts-pack.md`.
- ✅ **Script ready**: the full script is in the Shorts pack (S1–S12).
- ✏️ **To write**: the idea is in the pack. Write it with the same 4-step structure (hook → explain → tip → loop or CTA).

## Weekly rhythm

Each day of the week has its own theme so viewers learn what to expect:

| Day | Theme |
|---|---|
| Mon & Thu | **Psychology Effects #N** (a numbered series) |
| Tue | **Signs you…** |
| Wed | **Relationships** |
| Fri | **Quiz / interactive** (to get comments) |
| Sat | **Self-growth tip** |
| Sun | **Signs you…** / a series part |

**If you start mid or late October:** begin on the first Thursday you're ready. Thursday Oct 15, 22 or 29 all work. If you'd rather start on another weekday, just keep each day's theme and move the day numbers along.

**Before Day 1 (your prep week):** write and record the first 7–14 Shorts, so you start with a buffer and aren't editing the same day you post.

**Posting time:** choose one time and keep it every day. Early evening (5–7 pm in your audience's main time zone) is a good default. After 2 weeks, switch to the time YouTube Analytics shows under *"When your viewers are on YouTube."*

## Weekly production routine (about 3–4 hours a week)

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| **Sunday** | Write any ✏️ scripts for the coming week, and record every voiceover in one session |
| **Monday** | Make visuals and edit all 7 Shorts (captions, hook text in the first frame, music at low volume) |
| **Monday night** | Schedule all 7 in YouTube Studio with the same daily time |
| **Daily, 5 min** | Reply to comments in the first hour after posting, and pin a question comment |
| **Saturday, 10 min** | Check analytics (see below) |

Stay a week ahead. By Day 4 (Sunday), Week 2 should already be scheduled.

## What to check each Saturday

- **Viewed vs. swiped away** (Shorts feed): above ~70% viewed means the hook works. If it's low, rewrite the hook formula for that theme.
- **Average percentage viewed**: aim for 80% or more. If people leave in the middle, cut the explanation shorter.
- **Best theme of the week**: in the next cycle, give that theme a second weekly slot.
- **Comments**: turn common questions into new Short ideas.

## After the first 31 days

- Put the Shorts into playlists by series: *Psychology Effects*, *Signs You…*, *Relationships*, *Quick Quizzes*.
- Turn the top 3 Shorts into a "Part 2".
- Keep going with Effects #11 and onward (e.g. Dunning–Kruger, the halo effect, anchoring, the IKEA effect, the bystander effect).

# Inner Nook: "The Easy Child" Production Cuts

Two short cuts of the full script in `psych2go-analysis-and-script.md`. Every narration line is written to fit one 10-second clip (20–23 words).

---

## Cut A: 60-second test video (16:9, 720p, 6 clips)

| # | Role | Narration | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | Were you the kid who never caused trouble? Teachers called you mature, you did homework unasked, and you never needed anything. | Small blob sits quietly at a dinner table while two bigger blobs argue; it gives a thumbs-up |
| 2 | Build | That gold star had a hidden price. The easy child often learns early that their own needs quietly come second. | A gold "Low-Maintenance" star sticker lands on the blob's head; its own heart shrinks in the corner |
| 3 | Build | Sign one: asking for help feels like a burden. You type the message, reread it five times, then delete it. | Blob at a phone, typing "Hey, could you maybe…", deleting it |
| 4 | Build | Sign two: you read every room instantly. A sigh, a slammed door, a short text—your nervous system catches all of it. | Blob enters a room; small weather icons (storm, drizzle, sun) float above every other blob |
| 5 | Turn | Sign three: your answer is always "I'm fine." Psychologists link hiding feelings like this to lower well-being and weaker connection with others. | Friend blob cries over a tiny bandage; our blob hides a huge cast behind its back |
| 6 | Payoff | But you never had to be easy to deserve love. Which sign hit home? Tell the Nooklings below, and subscribe. | The star sticker peels off; a friend hands the blob a cup of tea and it smiles for real |
